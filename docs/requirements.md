## 1. Business & Requirements Documents

DocumentPurpose

**BRD – Business Requirements Document**
Why the application is being built and what business problem it solves.

**FRD – Functional Requirements Document**
Detailed functional requirements.

**NFR – Non-Functional Requirements**
Performance, security, scalability, availability, etc.

**User Stories**
Requirements from the user's perspective.

**Use Cases**
User/system interactions.

**Acceptance Criteria**
Conditions that must be satisfied for a feature to be accepted.

**Scope Document**
What's included/excluded from the project.

**Business Rules**
Rules and validations the application must follow.

Example flow:

```
BRD
 ↓
FRD
 ↓
User Stories
 ↓
Acceptance Criteria
```
